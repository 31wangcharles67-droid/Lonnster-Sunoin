# Lonnster Sonion: Game Plan

## Game type

Racing.

## Game idea

Lonnster Sonion is a quick racing game for teammates who want a fun challenge during practice breaks. The player races on one short track and tries to beat their own best time.

## Player

The player drives a small, bright race car. The car moves forward on its own. The player steers left and right, avoids trouble, and tries to reach the finish line as fast as possible.

## Core loop

1. Start the one track.
2. Steer around obstacles and enemies.
3. Reach the finish line.
4. See the finish time and top score.
5. Try the same track again to beat the fastest time.

## Win and lose

The race is over when the player reaches the finish line. The player wins the challenge by finishing with a faster time than their previous best.

The player loses time when they hit an obstacle or enemy. If the car crashes too much, it slows down, but the player can still finish and try again.

## Controls

- Left and right arrow keys steer the car.
- The space key gives the car a short boost.
- On phones, the player taps the left or right side of the screen to steer and taps a boost button for space.

## Version 1: one tiny level

Version 1 has one track designed to be fun for about 30 seconds. It has three simple sections: a straight start, a busy middle section, and a final sprint to the finish line.

### Obstacles and enemies

- **Road cones:** Stationary cones that make the player steer carefully.
- **Slow rival car:** Moves across part of the track and makes the player choose when to pass.
- **Puddle:** Slows the car briefly if the player drives through it.

### Scoring

The main score is the player’s finish time. A lower time is better. The game saves and shows the player’s top score, which is their fastest time on the track.

Using the boost at a good moment helps the player get a faster time. Hitting an obstacle adds a short time penalty.

### What makes it harder

The track starts easy so the player can learn the controls. Later in the same track, obstacles are placed closer together, the slow rival car moves more often, and the final section gives less room to steer. The challenge stays short and fair so players want to immediately try again.

## Version 1 success goal

After one play, a teammate should understand the controls, finish the track in about 30 seconds, see their top score, and want to replay it to beat their time.
